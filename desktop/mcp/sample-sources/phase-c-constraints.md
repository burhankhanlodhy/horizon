# Synthetic Service Records Policy

## Retention period

Retain complete synthetic financial records for exactly 7 years after the close of the reporting period.

## Legal hold

Records subject to a legal hold must not be deleted, even when the 7-year retention period has ended.

## Renewal notice

The sample service does not automatically renew. Either party must provide written notice at least 30 calendar days before the renewal date.

## Escalation

For this synthetic pilot, notify the on-call desk within 90 minutes of a record-access incident.
